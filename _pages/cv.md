Research experience
======
* **PhD researcher**, University of Leicester, 2023–2026
  * Screened *Drosophila* orthologues of human nystagmus- and albinism-associated genes for sleep and circadian phenotypes
  * Identified *hoe1*, *hoe2* and *lovit* as genes required for sleep and circadian rhythmicity
  * Mapped neurons downstream of photoreceptors using cell-type-specific RNAi, rescue, activity reporters and connectome analysis
* **Research assistant**, Biotechnology Lab, COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus, 2021–2022
  * NRPU-funded project (No. 8751) on nematicidal genes in *Pseudomonas koreensis* WZ005
  * Developed a DpnI-assisted methylome profiling (DAMP) method
  * Set up PCR, PAGE, SDS-PAGE and cloning in the laboratory
  * Supervised three BS and two MS students at the bench
* **Trainee researcher**, Health Biotech Lab, Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan, 2018–2019
  * Characterised rheumatoid arthritis-associated SNPs in silico
  * Contributed to association studies of *AFF3* and microRNA gene variants with rheumatoid arthritis
* **Undergraduate intern**, Microbiology Lab, Mardan Medical Complex, 2017
  * ELISA and real-time qPCR for detection of HBV and HCV; microscopy of blood and sputum slides
