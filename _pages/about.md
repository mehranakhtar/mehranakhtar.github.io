I am a *Drosophila* neurogeneticist. I study how signals from the visual system regulate sleep and circadian behaviour.

I completed my PhD in Genetics at the University of Leicester in 2026, in the Chen Lab, Division of Genetics and Genome Biology. My supervisors were Dr Ko-Fan Chen, Dr Mervyn Thomas and Professor Flaviano Giorgini. My thesis is titled *Lovit protein in photoreceptors regulates histamine-mediated* Drosophila *sleep and circadian rhythm*.

In my PhD I screened *Drosophila* orthologues of human genes associated with nystagmus and oculocutaneous albinism for sleep and circadian phenotypes. The screen identified *hoe1*, *hoe2* and *lovit* as genes required for sleep and circadian rhythmicity ([Akhtar et al., 2026, bioRxiv](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.27.728351)). *lovit* encodes a vesicular histamine transporter in photoreceptors. I now use fly genetics, behaviour and connectome analysis to identify the neurons downstream of photoreceptors that carry this signal to sleep circuits.

Before my PhD, I worked on the genetics of rheumatoid arthritis in a Pakistani cohort, in silico analysis of human coding and regulatory variants, and comparative genomics of a nematicidal bacterium.

I am seeking a postdoctoral position in *Drosophila* circuit or systems neuroscience.
