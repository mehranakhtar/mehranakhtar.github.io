Since 2019 I have worked as a freelance scientific editor alongside my research. I have edited more than 1,000 manuscripts in the life sciences and medicine, principally for authors who write in English as an additional language.

Services
------
* **Developmental editing:** structure of the manuscript, order and logic of the argument, and the links between paragraphs, so that each result is introduced by the question it answers.
* **Language editing:** grammar, sentence structure, terminology and register. Sentences that need correction are rewritten as whole sentences rather than patched.
* **Consistency and formatting:** abbreviations, units, gene and protein nomenclature, reference style and journal requirements.

How I work
------
* Edits are returned as tracked changes, so authors can accept or reject each change.
* Each manuscript receives a cover comment that summarises the revisions and inline comments that explain changes to terminology, request missing information and flag structural issues for the author to confirm.
* Manuscripts are edited in US or UK English, as the author or journal requires.
* I do not use AI tools to edit manuscripts.

Subject areas
------
All fields of the life sciences and medicine.
